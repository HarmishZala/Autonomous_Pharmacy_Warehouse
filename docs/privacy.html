JARVIS: Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

  • Who uses it: only its owner, Harmish Zala. No other users.
  • What it accesses: the owner's Google Calendar events, read or changed only when the owner asks.
  • Where data goes: requests and the calendar details needed to answer them are processed on the owner's computer, and are sent to the language-model provider (Groq) and a tracing service (LangSmith) to produce a reply. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Revoking access: Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.
  • Contact: harmishzala989@gmail.com
